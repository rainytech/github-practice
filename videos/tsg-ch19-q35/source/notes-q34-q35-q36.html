Solution:-
Working Notes:-
WN 1 — Salary to Proprietor (Shubho)
Salary to Shubho ₹ 15,000 is only provided, not paid, so it is not Drawings. It is treated like Interest on Capital. It is debited to Profit and Loss Account as a charge against profit. It is credited (added) to Shubho's Capital in the Balance Sheet. Only when Shubho actually withdraws it will it be recorded as Drawings.
WN 2 — Interest on Chatterji's Loan
Loan taken on 1st October, 2025 — period up to 31st March, 2026 = 6 months Interest = Loan × Rate100 × Period12 = 25,000 × 18100 × 612 = ₹ 2,250 (outstanding — not yet paid)
WN 3 — Transfer to General Reserve
Net Profit before transfer = 1,32,200 − 1,02,550 = ₹ 29,650 General Reserve = 29,650 × 10100 = ₹ 2,965 Net Profit transferred to Capital = 29,650 − 2,965 = ₹ 26,685
