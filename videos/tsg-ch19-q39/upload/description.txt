📘 THE 8 ADJUSTMENTS — WHERE EACH ONE GOES
(i) Closing Stock: cost ₹35,000, NRV ₹30,000 → value at ₹30,000 (lower). Trading Cr. side + Balance Sheet asset.
(ii) New machine ₹3,000 bought on credit, not recorded → add to Machinery; Creditor for Machinery ₹3,000 (liability).
(iii) Installation wages ₹500 → capital expenditure: deduct from Wages, add to Machinery (cost ₹18,100).
(iv) Further bad debts ₹300, provision raised to ₹1,400 → Debtors 43,800 − 300 − 1,400 = ₹42,100; P&L charge 1,000 + 300 + 1,400 − 800 = ₹1,900.
(v) Fire destroyed stock ₹8,000, insurance claim ₹5,000 admitted → Purchases − 8,000; Loss by fire ₹3,000 in P&L; Insurance Claim Receivable ₹5,000 (asset).
(vi) Outstanding wages ₹700, salaries ₹500 → added to expenses; shown as liabilities.
(vii) Prepaid insurance ₹250, advertisement ₹500 → deducted from expenses; shown as assets.
(viii) Depreciation: Machinery 18,100 × 10% = ₹1,810; Furniture 3,400 × 15% = ₹510; total ₹2,320 to P&L.

⚠️ 4 EXAM TRAPS
1. Closing stock at the lower of cost or NRV: ₹30,000, not ₹35,000.
2. Installation wages go to Machinery, so depreciation is on ₹18,100.
3. Loss by fire: deduct full ₹8,000 from purchases; only ₹3,000 is a loss; the ₹5,000 claim is an asset.
4. Old provision ₹800 is subtracted only in the P&L charge, never from debtors.
