T.S. Grewal Class 11 (CBSE, 2025–26 edition) · Chapter 8: Journal · Practical Problem 1 · Page 8.57
Transaction: 2023, April 3 — Goods purchased from Pankaj for credit ₹18,000

✅ JOURNAL ENTRY
Date: 2023, April 3
Purchases A/c … Dr. 18,000
   To Pankaj A/c 18,000
(Being goods purchased on credit from Pankaj)

📘 STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION
Two things happen in this transaction:
1️⃣ Goods come into the business → this is a PURCHASE.
2️⃣ Payment is made later → this is a CREDIT transaction.

When goods are bought for cash, we write "To Cash A/c".
When goods are bought on credit, we write the supplier's name — here, "To Pankaj A/c".
Why? Because the business must pay Pankaj ₹18,000 later. Writing his name records whom we owe.
Pankaj is now our CREDITOR.

📏 RULES APPLIED
• Purchases A/c — goods come in → Debit
• Pankaj A/c — Personal Account; Pankaj is the giver → "Credit the giver"
Modern approach: Purchases (expense) increases → Debit; Creditor (liability) increases → Credit.

⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES STUDENTS MAKE
✗ Writing "Goods A/c" — always use Purchases A/c for goods bought for resale.
✗ Writing "To Cash A/c" — no cash is paid on April 3; it is a credit purchase.
✗ Writing "To Creditors A/c" — in the journal, write the creditor's name: Pankaj A/c.
