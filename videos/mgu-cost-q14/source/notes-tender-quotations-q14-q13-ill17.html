Question 14 The accounts of National Ltd showed the following.
Particulars Amount (₹)
Direct materials 7,00,000
Direct wages 5,40,000
Works Overhead 1,62,000
Establishment overhead 1,12,000
What price should the company quote to manufacture a machine which will require an expenditure of ₹ 1,000 in material and ₹ 800 in wages so that it will yield a profit of 20% on selling price?
Solution
STEP 1 : COST SHEET
(National Ltd.)
Particulars Amount (₹)
Direct Materials 7,00,000
Add:Direct Wages 5,40,000
1. Prime Cost 12,40,000
Add:Works Overhead 1,62,000
2. Works Cost 14,02,000
Add:Establishment Overhead 1,12,000
3. Cost of Production 15,14,000
Working Notes (Step 2 — Overhead Absorption Rates & Profit Rate):
Sl.
No.		 Rate / Basis Formula & Step-by-Step Calculation Result
1 Works Overhead Rate
(on Direct Wages)		 = Works Overhead × 100Direct Wages = 1,62,000 × 1005,40,000 30%
2 Establishment Overhead Rate
(on Works Cost)		 = Establishment Overhead × 100Works Cost = 1,12,000 × 10014,02,000
= 7.9886% ≈ 8% (rounded off)		 8%
3 Rate of Profit
(Sales to Cost)		 Profit on Cost = 20100 – 20 × 100 = 2080 × 100 25%
(on Cost)
Technique — Converting Profit on Sales to Profit on Cost: If profit is 20% on Sales, it is equal to 20100 – 20 on Cost. General Formula:  Profit on Cost = Rate100 – Rate
Note — Standard Absorption Bases: When the question does not specify overhead recovery bases, Works Overhead is absorbed as a percentage of Direct Wages and Establishment (Administration) Overhead is absorbed as a percentage of Works Cost.
STEP 3 : STATEMENT SHOWING THE QUOTATION PRICE OF THE MACHINE
(National Ltd.)
Elements of Cost Amount (₹)
Direct Material 1,000.00
Add:Direct Wages 800.00
1. Prime Cost 1,800.00
Add:Works Overhead  (₹ 800 × 30100) 240.00
2. Works Cost 2,040.00
Add:Establishment Overhead  (₹ 2,040 × 8100) 163.20
3. Cost of Production (Total Cost) 2,203.20
Add:Profit (20% on Sales = 20100 – 20 on Cost) = ₹ 2,203.20 × 2080 550.80
Quotation / Selling Price 2,754.00

Illustration 17 The following figures have been obtained from the Books of Samsung India Ltd. for the year 2025.
Particulars Amount (₹)
Direct Material 6,00,000
Direct wages 5,00,000
Factory Overheads 3,00,000
Administration overheads 3,36,000
Selling overheads 2,24,000
Distribution overheads 1,40,000
Profit 4,20,000
A new model has been introduced in 2026 and the following expenses have been incurred.
Particulars Amount (₹)
Material 8,000
Wages 5,000
Assuming that in 2026, the rate of factory overhead has increased by 20%, the rate of distribution overhead has gone down by 10%, and selling and administration overheads rates have each gone up by 1212%, at what price should the product be sold so as to earn the same rate of profit on the selling price as in 2025? Factory overhead is based on Direct wages, while all other overheads are based on Factory Cost.
Solution
STEP 1 : COST SHEET
for the year 2025 (Samsung India Ltd.)
Particulars Amount (₹)
Direct Material 6,00,000
Add:Direct Wages 5,00,000
1. Prime Cost 11,00,000
Add:Factory Overheads 3,00,000
2. Works Cost (Factory Cost) 14,00,000
Add:Administration Overheads 3,36,000
3. Cost of Production 17,36,000
Add:Selling Overheads 2,24,000
Add:Distribution Overheads 1,40,000
4. Cost of Sales 21,00,000
Add:Profit 4,20,000
5. Sales 25,20,000
Working Notes (Step 2A — Calculation of Overhead Rates & Profit Rate for 2025):
Sl.
No.		 Rate / Basis Formula & Step-by-Step Calculation Result
1 Factory Overhead Rate
(on Direct Wages)		 = Factory Overheads × 100Direct Wages = 3,00,000 × 1005,00,000 60%
2 Administration Overhead Rate
(on Works Cost)		 = Administration Overheads × 100Works Cost = 3,36,000 × 10014,00,000 24%
3 Selling Overhead Rate
(on Works Cost)		 = Selling Overheads × 100Works Cost = 2,24,000 × 10014,00,000 16%
4 Distribution Overhead Rate
(on Works Cost)		 = Distribution Overheads × 100Works Cost = 1,40,000 × 10014,00,000 10%
5 Rate of Profit
(Sales to Cost)		 Profit on Sales = Profit × 100Sales = 4,20,000 × 10025,20,000 = 16.6667%
Profit on Cost = 16.6667100 – 16.6667 × 100 = 16.666783.3333 × 100		 20%
(on Cost)
Technique — Converting Profit on Sales to Profit on Cost: If profit is 16.6667% on Sales, it is equal to 16.6667100 – 16.6667 on Cost. General Formula:  Profit on Cost = Rate100 – Rate
Working Notes (Step 2B — Calculation of Revised Overhead Rates for 2026):
Sl.
No.		 Overhead & Change Revised Rate Calculation Revised
Rate
1 Factory Overhead
(Increased by 20%)		 = 60% × 100 + 20100 = 60 × 120100 72%
2 Administration Overhead
(Increased by 1212%)		 = 24% × 100 + 12.5100 = 24 × 112.5100 27%
3 Selling Overhead
(Increased by 1212%)		 = 16% × 100 + 12.5100 = 16 × 112.5100 18%
4 Distribution Overhead
(Decreased by 10%)		 = 10% × 100 – 10100 = 10 × 90100 9%
