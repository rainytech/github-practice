❓ QUESTION 14 (National Ltd)
Direct materials ₹7,00,000 · Direct wages ₹5,40,000 · Works overhead ₹1,62,000 · Establishment overhead ₹1,12,000.
What price should the company quote for a machine needing ₹1,000 material and ₹800 wages, to yield a profit of 20% on selling price?

✅ ANSWER: Quotation Price = ₹2,754.00

📘 STEP 1 — COST SHEET
Direct Materials 7,00,000 + Direct Wages 5,40,000 = Prime Cost 12,40,000
+ Works Overhead 1,62,000 = Works Cost 14,02,000
+ Establishment Overhead 1,12,000 = Cost of Production 15,14,000

📘 STEP 2 — RATES
1. Works Overhead rate (on Direct Wages) = 1,62,000 × 100 ⁄ 5,40,000 = 30%
2. Establishment Overhead rate (on Works Cost) = 1,12,000 × 100 ⁄ 14,02,000 = 7.9886% ≈ 8% (rounded off)
3. Profit 20% on Sales = 20 ⁄ (100 – 20) = 20 ⁄ 80 = 25% on Cost

📘 STEP 3 — QUOTATION PRICE OF THE MACHINE
Direct Material 1,000.00 + Direct Wages 800.00 = Prime Cost 1,800.00
+ Works Overhead (800 × 30%) 240.00 = Works Cost 2,040.00
+ Establishment Overhead (2,040 × 8%) 163.20 = Cost of Production 2,203.20
+ Profit (2,203.20 × 20 ⁄ 80) 550.80 = Quotation Price 2,754.00

⚠️ EXAM TRAPS
✗ Taking 20% of cost as profit gives ₹2,643.84 — wrong. Profit is on selling price, so use 20 ⁄ 80 on cost.
✓ Check: 550.80 is exactly 20% of 2,754.00.
✗ Wrong base: Works overhead goes on direct wages; establishment overhead goes on works cost.
✗ Writing 8% with no working: show the formula and write "7.9886% ≈ 8% (rounded off)".
