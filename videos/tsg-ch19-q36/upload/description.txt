📘 THE 6 ADJUSTMENTS
(i) Closing Stock ₹2,25,000.
(ii) Machinery ₹45,000 shown as Purchases, freight ₹5,000 in Freight on Purchases → Purchases 15,95,000 − 45,000 = 15,50,000; Freight 25,000 − 5,000 = 20,000; Machinery 5,00,000 + 50,000 = 5,50,000.
(iii) Commission 2.5% on corrected sales 22,00,000 = ₹55,000; paid 45,000, so ₹10,000 outstanding.
(iv) Investments (cost 1,00,000) sold at 10% profit, proceeds 1,10,000 credited to Sales → Sales 23,10,000 − 1,10,000 = 22,00,000; profit ₹10,000 to P&L; investments Nil.
(v) Further bad debts 10,000; provision 5% of 2,40,000 = 12,000 → P&L charge 15,000 + 10,000 + 12,000 − 8,000 = ₹29,000; Debtors ₹2,28,000.
(vi) Depreciation: Building 2.5% = 7,500; Machinery 50,000 (old) + 2,500 (new machine, 6 months) = 52,500; Furniture 4,000; total ₹64,000.
