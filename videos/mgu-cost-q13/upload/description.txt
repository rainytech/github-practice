❓ QUESTION 13 (Alpha Manufacturing Company, year 2025)
Materials used ₹3,00,000 · Direct wages ₹2,40,000 · Factory overhead ₹60,000 · Administration overhead ₹30,000.
Prepare a cost sheet and calculate the price to quote for a machine requiring materials ₹2,500 and direct wages ₹1,500, so that the price may yield a profit of 20% on the selling price.

✅ ANSWER: Quotation Price = ₹5,742.19 (rounded off: ₹5,742)

📘 STEP 1 — COST SHEET
Materials used 3,00,000 + Direct Wages 2,40,000 = Prime Cost 5,40,000
+ Factory Overhead 60,000 = Works Cost 6,00,000
+ Administration Overhead 30,000 = Cost of Production 6,30,000

📘 STEP 2 — RATES
1. Factory Overhead rate (on Direct Wages) = 60,000 × 100 ⁄ 2,40,000 = 25%
2. Administration Overhead rate (on Works Cost) = 30,000 × 100 ⁄ 6,00,000 = 5%
3. Profit 20% on Sales = 20 ⁄ (100 – 20) = 20 ⁄ 80 = 25% on Cost

📘 STEP 3 — QUOTATION PRICE OF THE MACHINE
Materials 2,500.00 + Direct Wages 1,500.00 = Prime Cost 4,000.00
+ Factory Overhead (1,500 × 25%) 375.00 = Works Cost 4,375.00
+ Administration Overhead (4,375 × 5%) 218.75 = Cost of Production 4,593.75
+ Profit (4,593.75 × 20 ⁄ 80) 1,148.44 = Quotation Price 5,742.19 (rounded off: ₹5,742)

💡 EXAM TIPS
1. Different names, same idea: Factory OH = Works OH (on direct wages); Administration OH = Establishment OH (on works cost).
2. Rounding: profit 1,148.4375 → write ₹1,148.44; show the final price rounded off: ₹5,742.
✓ Check: 1,148.44 is 20% of 5,742.19.
