Final accounts of Ashok with "Purchases (Adjusted)" and 5 adjustments: Trading Account, Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet as at 31st March, 2026 — explained by Rainy Goodwill. Pause after the question and try it yourself first!

✅ ANSWER (matches the textbook)
Gross Profit ₹2,31,000 · Net Profit ₹2,00,300 · Balance Sheet total ₹2,52,800 (tallied ✓)

🪤 THE BIG TRAP — ITEMS ALREADY ADJUSTED
• Purchases (Adjusted) = Opening Stock + Purchases − Purchases Return − Closing Stock. So the Trading Account shows no Opening Stock and no Closing Stock.
• Closing Stock ₹40,000 is inside the Trial Balance → Balance Sheet (Assets) only.
• Wages Outstanding ₹8,000 → Liabilities only; Prepaid Rent ₹3,000 → Assets only. Never adjust them twice.

📘 THE 5 ADJUSTMENTS
(i) Salaries 5,000 + 1,000 outstanding − 800 paid in advance = ₹5,200 to P&L; Outstanding Salaries ₹1,000 (liability), Prepaid Salaries ₹800 (asset).
(ii) Insurance 5,000 − 2,000 prepaid = ₹3,000 to P&L; Prepaid Insurance ₹2,000 (asset).
(iii) Depreciation: Building 1,50,000 × 5% = ₹7,500; Furniture 60,000 × 10% = ₹6,000; total ₹13,500 to P&L. Provision for Depreciation on Furniture 12,000 + 6,000 = ₹18,000 → Furniture 60,000 − 18,000 = ₹42,000.
(iv) Goods ₹1,500 taken by Ashok → Purchases (Adjusted) 2,90,000 − 1,500 = ₹2,88,500; Drawings 5,000 + 1,500 = ₹6,500.
(v) Provision for Doubtful Debts 10% of 25,000 = ₹2,500 → P&L: Bad Debts 2,000 + 2,500 = ₹4,500; Debtors 25,000 − 2,500 = ₹22,500.

📗 TRADING ACCOUNT
Purchases (Adjusted) 2,88,500 · Wages 10,000 · Gross Profit 2,31,000 | Sales 5,29,500

📗 PROFIT & LOSS ACCOUNT
Salaries 5,200 · Bad Debts & Provision 4,500 · Rent 5,000 · Insurance 3,000 · Depreciation 13,500 · Net Profit 2,00,300 | Gross Profit 2,31,000 + Apprentice Premium 500 = 2,31,500

📗 BALANCE SHEET (₹2,52,800)

Assets: Building 1,42,500 · Furniture 42,000 · Closing Stock 40,000 · Sundry Debtors 22,500 · Prepaid Rent 3,000 · Prepaid Salaries 800 · Prepaid Insurance 2,000
