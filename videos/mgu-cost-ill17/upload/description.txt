❓ ILLUSTRATION 17 (Samsung India Ltd, year 2025)
Direct Material ₹6,00,000 · Direct wages ₹5,00,000 · Factory Overheads ₹3,00,000 · Administration overheads ₹3,36,000 · Selling overheads ₹2,24,000 · Distribution overheads ₹1,40,000 · Profit ₹4,20,000.
A new model introduced in 2026 needs Material ₹8,000 and Wages ₹5,000.
In 2026 the factory overhead rate goes up by 20%, the distribution overhead rate goes down by 10%, and the selling and administration overhead rates each go up by 12½%.
At what price should the product be sold to earn the same rate of profit on the selling price as in 2025?
Factory overhead is based on direct wages; all other overheads are based on factory cost.

✅ ANSWER: Estimated Selling Price = ₹30,677

📘 STEP 1 — COST SHEET FOR 2025
Direct Material 6,00,000 + Direct Wages 5,00,000 = Prime Cost 11,00,000
+ Factory Overheads 3,00,000 = Works Cost (Factory Cost) 14,00,000
+ Administration Overheads 3,36,000 = Cost of Production 17,36,000
+ Selling 2,24,000 + Distribution 1,40,000 = Cost of Sales 21,00,000
+ Profit 4,20,000 = Sales 25,20,000

📘 STEP 2A — RATES FOR 2025
1. Factory OH (on Direct Wages) = 3,00,000 × 100 ⁄ 5,00,000 = 60%
2. Administration OH (on Works Cost) = 3,36,000 × 100 ⁄ 14,00,000 = 24%
3. Selling OH (on Works Cost) = 2,24,000 × 100 ⁄ 14,00,000 = 16%
4. Distribution OH (on Works Cost) = 1,40,000 × 100 ⁄ 14,00,000 = 10%
5. Profit on Sales = 4,20,000 × 100 ⁄ 25,20,000 = 16.6667% → on Cost = 16.6667 ⁄ (100 – 16.6667) × 100 = 20%

📘 STEP 2B — REVISED RATES FOR 2026
Factory OH 60 × 120 ⁄ 100 = 72% · Administration 24 × 112.5 ⁄ 100 = 27% · Selling 16 × 112.5 ⁄ 100 = 18% · Distribution 10 × 90 ⁄ 100 = 9%

📘 STEP 3 — ESTIMATED PRICE OF THE NEW MODEL (2026)
Direct Material 8,000 + Direct Wages 5,000 = Prime Cost 13,000
+ Factory OH (5,000 × 72%) 3,600 = Works Cost (Factory Cost) 16,600
+ Administration OH (16,600 × 27%) 4,482 = Cost of Production 21,082
+ Selling OH (16,600 × 18%) 2,988 + Distribution OH (16,600 × 9%) 1,494 = Cost of Sales 25,564
+ Profit (20% on cost: 25,564 × 20 ⁄ 100 = 5,112.80, rounded off) 5,113 = Estimated Selling Price ₹30,677

💡 EXAM TIPS
1. Check the base: factory OH on direct wages; administration, selling and distribution OH on factory cost (not cost of production).
2. "Increased by 20%" does not mean add 20: 60% × 120 ⁄ 100 = 72%, not 80%.
3. 16.6667% on sales = 20% on cost, so profit = 25,564 × 20% = ₹5,112.80 → ₹5,113.
