A new model, and every overhead rate has changed. Find its selling price at the same rate of profit as last year.

❓ ILLUSTRATION 17 (Samsung India Ltd, year 2025)
Direct Material ₹6,00,000 · Direct wages ₹5,00,000 · Factory Overheads ₹3,00,000 · Administration overheads ₹3,36,000 · Selling overheads ₹2,24,000 · Distribution overheads ₹1,40,000 · Profit ₹4,20,000.
New model in 2026: Material ₹8,000 · Wages ₹5,000.
In 2026 the factory overhead rate has increased by 20%, the distribution overhead rate has gone down by 10%, and selling and administration overhead rates have each gone up by 12½%.
At what price should the product be sold so as to earn the same rate of profit on the selling price as in 2025?
Factory overhead is based on direct wages; all other overheads are based on factory cost.

✅ ANSWER: Estimated Selling Price = ₹30,677

📘 STEP 1 — COST SHEET 2025
Direct Material 6,00,000 + Direct Wages 5,00,000 = Prime Cost 11,00,000
+ Factory Overheads 3,00,000 = Works Cost (Factory Cost) 14,00,000
+ Administration Overheads 3,36,000 = Cost of Production 17,36,000
+ Selling 2,24,000 + Distribution 1,40,000 = Cost of Sales 21,00,000
+ Profit 4,20,000 = Sales 25,20,000

📘 STEP 2A — RATES FOR 2025
Factory OH = 3,00,000 × 100 ⁄ 5,00,000 = 60% of direct wages
Administration OH = 3,36,000 × 100 ⁄ 14,00,000 = 24% of works cost
Selling OH = 2,24,000 × 100 ⁄ 14,00,000 = 16% of works cost
Distribution OH = 1,40,000 × 100 ⁄ 14,00,000 = 10% of works cost
Profit = 4,20,000 × 100 ⁄ 25,20,000 = 16⅔% on sales = 16⅔ ⁄ (100 – 16⅔) = 20% on cost

📘 STEP 2B — REVISED RATES FOR 2026
Factory OH 60% × 120 ⁄ 100 = 72% · Administration OH 24% × 112.5 ⁄ 100 = 27%
Selling OH 16% × 112.5 ⁄ 100 = 18% · Distribution OH 10% × 90 ⁄ 100 = 9%

📘 STEP 3 — ESTIMATED PRICE OF THE NEW MODEL
Direct Material 8,000 + Direct Wages 5,000 = Prime Cost 13,000
+ Factory OH (5,000 × 72%) 3,600 = Works Cost 16,600
+ Administration OH (16,600 × 27%) 4,482 = Cost of Production 21,082
+ Selling OH (16,600 × 18%) 2,988 + Distribution OH (16,600 × 9%) 1,494 = Cost of Sales 25,564
+ Profit (25,564 × 20 ⁄ 100 = 5,112.80, rounded off) 5,113 = Estimated Selling Price 30,677

💡 EXAM TIPS
1. "Increased by 20%" means 20% of the old rate: 60% × 120 ⁄ 100 = 72%, not 60% + 20% = 80%.
2. Read the basis: only factory OH is on direct wages; administration, selling and distribution OH are all on factory cost (₹16,600).
3. 16⅔% on sales = 20% on cost. Quick check: 4,20,000 ⁄ 21,00,000 = 20%. So profit is exactly ₹5,112.80; show it rounded off as ₹5,113.
