T.S. Grewal Class 11 (CBSE, 2025–26 edition) · Chapter 8: Journal · Practical Problem 1 · Page 8.57
Transaction: 2023, April 16 — Rent paid to Amit ₹5,000

✅ JOURNAL ENTRY
Date: 2023, April 16
Rent A/c … Dr. 5,000
   To Cash A/c 5,000
(Being rent paid to Amit)

📘 STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATION
Amit is only the landlord — the owner of the building we use.
We pay ₹5,000 in cash for the use of his building. That is RENT, an expense of the business.
1️⃣ Rent is an expense → Rent A/c is debited.
2️⃣ Cash goes out of the business → Cash A/c is credited.
Amit is paid in full. He owes us nothing, and we owe him nothing — so Amit's account does not appear in this entry.

📏 RULES APPLIED
• Rent A/c — Nominal Account → "Debit all expenses and losses"
• Cash A/c — Real Account → "Credit what goes out"
Modern approach: Expense increases → Debit; Asset (Cash) decreases → Credit.

⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES STUDENTS MAKE
✗ Debiting "Amit A/c" — Amit is not our debtor. We are paying for rent, not lending money to Amit.
✗ Crediting "Amit A/c" — we do not owe Amit anything; the rent is paid in cash.
✓ Write a person's name in the entry only when he owes us, or we owe him.
