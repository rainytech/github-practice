Privacy Policy

Goodwill Tuition Centre, Ernakulam, Kerala, India · Last updated 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information Goodwill Tuition Centre ("we") handles on this website and in our own video upload tool, "Goodwill Video Uploader".

1. This website

This website gives information about our online Accountancy and Cost Accounting classes.

When you contact us on WhatsApp, we use your name, phone number and messages only to answer your enquiry and arrange classes.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

2. Goodwill Video Uploader and YouTube API Services

Goodwill Video Uploader is a private tool used only by our teacher on our own computer.

It uses YouTube API Services to upload our own educational videos to our own YouTube channel and to add them to our own playlists.

It signs in only to our own YouTube channel through Google's secure sign-in (OAuth).

It does not collect, store or share any information about viewers, students or other YouTube users.

The sign-in token is stored only on our own computer and is never shared.

By using our YouTube channel and videos, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Access given to the tool can be removed at any time from the Google security settings page.

3. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
